      How to play

      Each day one FDA-approved drug is the answer. The figure is its plasma concentration over time after the dose noted beneath it, and the drawing inside is its structure. You have eleven guesses. Each miss adds a sampling time to the figure, sharpens the structure and opens one entry of the dossier. Under a guess, bold marks what it shares with the answer, and an arrow shows whether the answer’s half-life is longer or shorter.
