        How to play

        One FDA-approved drug a day. The ring is its plasma concentration after a dose: time runs clockwise, concentration outward. You have eight guesses. Each miss opens a clue and sharpens the structure. Under a guess, bold marks what it shares with the answer, and the arrow by the year points toward the answer’s.
